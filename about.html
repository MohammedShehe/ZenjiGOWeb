About ZenjiGO

Moving Zanzibar smarter — one ride at a time.

Our story

ZenjiGO was born from a simple idea: make getting around Zanzibar as easy and reliable as the island is beautiful. Whether you need a quick boda to the market, a bajaji for the family, or a comfortable taxi to the airport, we connect you with verified local drivers instantly.

We built the platform for both riders and drivers — transparent fares, live tracking, flexible payments, and fair earnings. Our technology is designed for the realities of island life while meeting modern expectations for safety and convenience.
